About ISTST

Indian Society of Thoracic Surgeons & Trust (ISTST) plays a significant role in advancing thoracic surgery in India by fostering education and skills development among young surgeons through their annual congress, ISTSCON.

Our Mission

Empowering Surgeons, Advancing Thoracic Care, and Enhancing Patient Outcomes Through Education, Collaboration, and Innovation in the Field of Thoracic Surgery Across India.

Our mission is to advance the field of thoracic surgery by providing surgeons with education, fostering collaboration, and driving innovation, ultimately improving patient care and outcomes across India.

Our Vision

To Lead the Advancement of Thoracic Surgery in India, Elevating Standards of Care, Inspiring Innovation, and Improving Health Outcomes for Patients Nationwide.

Our vision is to be the premier organization advancing thoracic surgery in India, driving innovation, fostering excellence, and improving healthcare standards to positively impact patient outcomes across the nation.

Aim and Objectives: Elevating Surgical Standards

Education & Training: Provide access to advanced resources and hands-on training.

Annual Congress: Organize ISTSCON for global expert engagement.

Research & Innovation: Encourage research to improve patient outcomes.
